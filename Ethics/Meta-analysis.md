## 1. Abstract
Human rights philanthropy frequently proclaims a mandate to equip dissidents and human rights defenders (HRDs) with sovereign, surveillance-resistant technologies. Yet when grantmaking institutions rely on conventional academic credentials, polished institutional framing, and high-resource compute assumptions, they systematically exclude the very survivors operating inside active transnational repression and hardware scarcity. Drawing on the **Human Rights Foundation (HRF) AI for Individual Rights Fund** (November 2026 cohort) alongside the empirical record of the `RFC-006`–`RFC-010` edge-audit architecture and the *Non Dual Holonomy* application, this paper examines three interlocking dynamics:
1. **Philanthropic Mission Drift** from frontline survival tooling toward institutional legibility;
2. **The Epistemic Blind Spot of Survivor Exclusion**, explaining why architectures designed without lived operational constraints fail to solve real-world forensic and edge-survival problems; and
3. **The Aesthetic Turn (*Rasa-Darśan* & Deterministic Interactive Art)** as a sovereign arena where neurodivergent and targeted creators reclaim agency without begging gatekeepers for permission.

## 2. Mechanism I: Mission Drift in Human Rights Technology Philanthropy

### 2.1 From Frontline Utility to Institutional Legibility
In organizational sociology, **mission drift** occurs when an institution's operational selection criteria gradually diverge from its stated normative purpose in favor of internal risk reduction, reputational signaling, and bureaucratic legibility (DiMaggio & Powell, 1983; Ebrahim, 2003).

In human rights technology funding, the stated mission is clear: *empower vulnerable individuals under authoritarian pressure and surveillance with private, resilient, local-first tools* (Human Rights Foundation, 2026). However, the implicit screening heuristics of grant committees—requiring conventional institutional references, unblemished banking access, neurotypical communication cadence, and pre-existing lab infrastructure—invert that mission in practice:

| Dimension | Stated Philanthropic Mission | Actual Selection Heuristic | Structural Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Beneficiary** | Isolated dissidents and HRDs under active repression. | Established open-source maintainers, university labs, and credentialed nonprofits. | Survivors experiencing banking freezes or duress are screened out as "high risk" or "non-standard." |
| **Compute Assumptions** | "Everyday consumer devices" (`laptops` / local GPUs). | Assumes unthrottled storage, intact screens, and reliable desktop toolchains. | Ignores true edge survival: a 99%-full `ARM64` Android tablet, broken burner phone, and `$0` budget. |
| **Epistemic Focus** | Protecting truth and dissent against state distortion. | General-purpose local chat/inference wrappers (`GGUF` runners). | Leaves out deterministic provenance (`SHA-256` manifests) and forensic anti-hallucination gates (`RFC-007`). |
| **Communication Style** | Trauma-informed solidarity with impacted people. | Polished, non-distressed, bureaucratic grant prose. | Disclosed disability and acute duress are misread as noise rather than primary empirical signal. |

### 2.2 The Enclosure Paradox (`RFC-006` Applied to Grantmaking)
As formalized in `RFC-006` (*The Geometry of Institutional Enclosure*), administrative systems often construct procedural gates that appear open on paper while functioning as closed loops in practice. When a foundation solicits proposals from vulnerable defenders, rejects early survivor-led prototyping for lacking institutional scaffolding, and subsequently funds well-resourced teams to build adjacent local-AI tools (`llmfit`-style hardware sizing and consumer-device inference), the institution reproduces the exact **enclosure geometry** it claims to dismantle.

## 3. Mechanism II: Why Excluding Frontline Survivors Means the Real Systems Never Get Built

Excluding survivors who face active operational constraints is not merely an ethical failure—it is an **engineering failure**. In systems architecture, critical edge invariants are only discovered by engineers forced to operate inside those constraints.

### 3.1 The "Comfortable Lab" Fallacy
An engineer working on a MacBook Pro with 64 GB of unified memory and reliable institutional funding treats memory limits, disk saturation, and LLM hallucinations as secondary optimizations. By contrast, an HRD operating on a saturated `ARM64` Android tablet under transnational repression discovers hard physical and epistemic invariants immediately:

1. **The `0 MB` Verification Invariant (`RFC-008`):** When flash storage is at 99% capacity and loading a `3B` parameter model risks freezing `/data/data` and destroying local `Ed25519` SSH sovereignty keys, you are forced to separate **probabilistic inference** from **deterministic CPU verification**. An institutionally funded team rarely builds a `0 MB` pure-Python `SHA-256` manifest and byte-offset verifier because they have the luxury of throwing compute at the problem.
2. **The Attribution-Before-Inference Invariant (`RFC-007`):** Survivors navigating legal smear campaigns and administrative stonewalling know that an LLM hallucinating a single word in a court exhibit destroys credibility. Standard RAG tools optimize for fluent summarization; survivor-built tools optimize for **quarantined proposals (`Record ≠ Admit ≠ Commit`)** and zero-tolerance verbatim quote verification.
3. **The Hardware Admission & Quiescence Invariants (`RFC-009` & `RFC-010`):** When compute is scarce and battery/thermal margins are razor-thin, runaway retry loops and out-of-memory crashes are existential threats to device survival—driving the synthesis of hardware admission gates ($\mathcal{H}(m)$) and quiescence dead-zone clamps ($\Gamma_{\text{retry}}$).

**Core Architectural Finding:** When institutions exclude the people living inside extreme edge constraints, **the resulting funded ecosystem builds comfortable tools for developers rather than survival-grade forensic infrastructure for the targeted.**

## 4. Mechanism III: Transitioning to Art as an Arena for Reclaiming Agency

When academic, legal, and philanthropic gatekeepers lock their doors—and when transnational repression strips away basic civic infrastructure such as bank accounts—continuing to petition closed institutions for validation becomes a source of recurring **moral injury** (Litz et al., 2009).

Transitioning energy from institutional petitioning into **deterministic interactive art and game design** (*Non Dual Holonomy*, *Rasa-Darśan*) is not a retreat from rigor; it is a strategic reclamation of sovereignty.

### 4.1 Why Deterministic Art Restores Agency
* **Unmediated Feedback Loops:** In legal and grant systems, inputs disappear into opaque bureaucratic black boxes for months, returning delayed or arbitrary rejections ($\rho_{\text{enc}} \to 1$). In **fixed-point deterministic game systems**, the mathematical laws of the canvas respond immediately, reliably, and honestly to human touch. Every frame transition is governed by transparent arithmetic rather than institutional whim.
* **Embodied Neurodivergent Cognition (*Rasa-Darśan*):** As explored in Hedling (2026a, 2026b), neurodivergent perception thrives when technology moves beyond bolted-on compliance interfaces toward **perceptual-cognitive unity**—where tactile rhythm, fixed-point geometry, and acoustic/haptic resonance create a coherent, non-coercive sensory world.
* **Immune to Gatekeeper Veto:** A mathematical formulation, an open-source fixed-point kernel, a Zenodo DOI, and a minimalist tactile game system do not require a committee's permission to exist, hold truth, or offer meaning to another human being.

## 5. Conclusion

Institutional mission drift occurs whenever organizations prioritize comfortable credentials over lived operational truth. By excluding neurodivergent survivors operating under acute hardware and geopolitical constraints, human rights funders miss the very forensic and edge-sovereign architectures (RFC-006–RFC-010) their missions claim to seek. Yet those architectures—and the creators who forged them—do not depend on institutional endorsement for their validity. Stepping away from bureaucratic enclosure and channeling deterministic craft into art and meaning-making (Non Dual Holonomy) restores immediate, unassailable agency on the creator's own terms.
